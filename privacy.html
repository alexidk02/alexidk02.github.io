classroom-sync privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What the app reads

With the user's permission, classroom-sync reads, read-only:

  • the user's Google Classroom courses, assignments, course materials and announcements;
  • the Google Drive files attached to those posts.

It never creates, changes or deletes anything in Google Classroom or Google Drive.

Where the data goes

Everything the app reads is saved only on the user's own computer, in their personal notes. Nothing is sent to any server, shared with other people, sold or used for advertising. The sign-in token is stored only on that computer.

Who uses it

The app has a single user, its author. It does not collect data about anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local folder removes all saved data.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of the app.
